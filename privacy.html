How this website handles inquiries and optional measurement.

Updated October 22, 2026.

Contacting Verdeland Homes

Phone and email links let you contact VerdeLand Home Builders directly. We use the information you send to respond to your inquiry. The website email is info@verdelandhomebuilders.com. Please avoid sending sensitive documents through an initial inquiry.

Property inquiry form

The form sends your name, email address, phone number, selected inquiry type and question to info@verdelandhomebuilders.com. We use these details to respond. Resend processes the message for delivery using info@keystonepinnacle.com as the service sender and your email address as the reply address. Inquiry content is processed by Resend and the receiving email system.

The confirmation means our email service accepted the message for delivery. It does not confirm that the message reached an inbox. The form does not subscribe you to marketing email. Please do not include financial, identity or other sensitive documents.

Form protection

Short-lived form tokens, a spam field and rate limits help prevent misuse. Upstash stores technical rate-limit records under a secret-key hash of your IP address, plus request identifiers and message digests for duplicate protection. These records do not contain your name, email address, phone number or question text. Rate-limit records expire after 15 minutes or 24 hours; duplicate-protection records expire after 24 hours. If a required protection or delivery service is unavailable, the form does not report a successful submission.

Essential functions

The site remembers your privacy choice in your browser for up to 180 days. The private-preview access setting is separate. Hosting services process technical request data needed to provide and protect the website.

Optional visit analytics

With your permission, Google Analytics measures page visits, property views, clicks on our phone and email links, inquiry starts and submissions accepted by the email service. These events use browser identifiers, property identifiers and contact categories. Inquiry-start and accepted-submission events include only a general-question category; they do not include the form answers, selected inquiry type or delivery receipt. An accepted-submission event does not prove inbox delivery. We do not send names, email addresses, phone numbers or message text in these events. Page addresses sent to Google omit query strings and fragments, and the referring page address is not included. Advertising features stay off.

Google Analytics cookies are configured to expire after 180 days without extending their lifetime on each visit. Google processes analytics data under its privacy policy. The buyer guides and this privacy page do not load analytics.

Session replay

Microsoft Clarity is not enabled. The session-replay preference is unavailable until that service has been configured and disclosed. Inquiry pages are excluded from session replay.

Manage your choices

Optional analytics stay off until you agree. Rejecting them does not prevent you from browsing or contacting us. Open Cookie preferences below to change your choice. Withdrawing permission unloads optional tracking by refreshing the page and clears its accessible first-party cookies. It does not erase information already received by a provider.

We honor Global Privacy Control by keeping optional tracking off. Browser identifiers estimate visits and use of a browser; they do not establish a visitor’s personal identity.
