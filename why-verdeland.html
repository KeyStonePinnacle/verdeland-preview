Why VerdeLand

A family tradition, built forward.

Homebuilding was part of my life long before VerdeLand. My father spent more than thirty years in the business, and my uncle built homes as well. Watching them taught me that a builder’s reputation is earned one house — and one homeowner — at a time.

I founded VerdeLand in 2001 to carry that tradition forward in my own name. Since then, VerdeLand has completed more than 130 recorded sales in Nassau County — every one in the public record, and I’ll point you to them.

Warranty

What you're covered for.

Homes we build are sold with a written builder's limited warranty. In broad terms it covers workmanship, materials and design for the first year; the plumbing, electrical and HVAC systems for two; and major structural elements — foundation, framing, load-bearing walls — for six.

It's worth comparing that to buying an older house, where whatever goes wrong after closing is yours.

Coverage has limits and exclusions, and the terms are set out in the executed warranty for each home. We'll give you the full document to read with your attorney before you sign anything.
