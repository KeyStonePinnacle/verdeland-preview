Why VerdeLand

A family tradition, built forward.

Homebuilding was part of my life long before VerdeLand. My father spent more than thirty years in the business, and my uncle built homes as well. Watching them taught me that a builder’s reputation is earned one house — and one homeowner — at a time.

I founded VerdeLand in 2001 to carry that tradition forward in my own name. Since then, VerdeLand has completed more than 130 recorded sales in Nassau County — every one in the public record, and I’ll point you to them.

Steve, on a job site Ordinary clothes. Unposed. Middle distance. This single photograph does more for the "inviting" goal than anything else on the site.

How it works

One builder.

VerdeLand is me. I handle the lots, the plans, the permits, the site and the finishes. There's no sales department and no project manager between us — if something about one of my houses isn't right, it's mine to fix.

When a home is for sale, a licensed real estate agent handles the listing, showings and paperwork. Email or call us with a question, and we'll put you in touch with the right person.

If you have a property you're thinking about selling, you talk to me directly.
