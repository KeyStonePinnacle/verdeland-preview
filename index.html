Why VerdeLand

A family tradition, built forward.

My father spent more than thirty years in the business, and my uncle built homes as well. I founded VerdeLand in 2001 to carry that tradition forward in my own name — more than 130 recorded sales in Nassau County since.

The record is public — every home in our portfolio can be verified in deed and MLS records — and every home is sold with a written builder’s limited warranty.
