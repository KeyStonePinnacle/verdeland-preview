118 Meacham Avenue

Elmont, New York

$1,295,000

This house sits on a 0.19-acre lot on a residential street in Elmont. The main floor is laid out around a kitchen that opens to the family room, with a separate dining room that can be closed off.

There's a mudroom off the garage with a bench and hooks, which is the kind of thing that doesn't photograph well and matters every single day.

Upstairs: four bedrooms, including a primary with two closets and a bath with a separate shower. The laundry is on the bedroom floor. The lower level is finished with eight-foot ceilings and full-size windows.

Completed in 2025.
