What you can choose

Buyers select finishes on homes we’re building. That is not the same as a custom build, and we’d rather be clear about the difference.

On a recent home the buyer selected just over $70,000 of finish upgrades — a Wolf range, panel-integrated Sub-Zero refrigeration, a microwave built into the cabinetry, and stained hardwood throughout.

What you’re covered for

Homes we build are sold with a written builder’s limited warranty: workmanship, materials and design for the first year; plumbing, electrical and HVAC for two; major structural elements for six.

Worth comparing to buying an older house, where whatever goes wrong after closing is yours.

Coverage has limits and exclusions. The executed warranty for each home governs, and we’ll give you the full document to read with your attorney.
