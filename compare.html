How C was assembled

Four agents judged A and B independently — buyer trust, visual craft, structure & conversion, and mobile at 390px. All four converged on the same shape: A's skeleton and warmth wearing B's components and proof. Full verdicts and the two conflict rulings are in docs/18.

What to judge, in order
  1. In two seconds, which one tells you what this company does?
  2. Which feels like a $1.3M house — and which feels like it's trying to?
  3. B shows one home large; A shows three. Which would you rather land on?
  4. B's project story explains a decision. Is that worth the extra scroll?
  5. Which reads as a builder, and which as a marketing department?
  6. Check both at mobile width — that's where most people will arrive.
