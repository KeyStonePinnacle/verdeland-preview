How the purchase works

We buy with our own money.

No lender. No underwriting. No financing contingency. Nothing about the sale depends on a bank saying yes, and there's no third party who can change the terms late or walk away.

Plenty of buyers say “cash.” It's a fair thing to ask anyone to prove — including us. Ask for proof of funds and we'll show you ours.

What happens next

What happens to your property.

If we buy it, we build on it. The existing house comes down, and a new single-family home goes up in its place — the same kind of house you can see in our portfolio.

For a lot of the people we talk to, that's the part that matters. If you're selling a house your family lived in, the plan isn't a flip or a hold. A new house goes up, and someone is going to live there.

What we look at

  • Vacant land
  • Older single-family homes
  • Bungalows
  • Estate properties
  • Oversized lots
  • Homes past practical repair

Every property is different. If you're not sure whether yours fits, write in anyway — it costs you nothing to ask.

What happens when you write in

1

You tell us about the property.

Address, roughly what's there, and anything you think we should know. A few sentences is plenty.

2

We look into it.

We check the lot, the zoning, and what could be built there. That usually takes a few days.

3

We come back to you honestly.

If it's something we can build on, we'll say so and explain what a conversation would look like. There's no financing to arrange on our side, so what we agree is what closes. If it isn't something we can use, we'll tell you that too, and why.

You'll be talking to the builder

VerdeLand is one person. Property messages come straight to me — not to a salesperson, not to a call center. There's no obligation on either side, and reaching out isn't an offer to buy or an agreement to sell.

What we can't promise

We can't promise that we'll buy your property, that it can be subdivided, that it will get zoning approval, or that any particular development is possible on it. Those things depend on the property's own conditions and on the municipality, and nobody who tells you otherwise before looking is being honest with you.

What we can promise is that we'll look carefully and tell you what we find.

Tell us about your property

A few details are enough to start. If you'd rather not give the exact address yet, a general location is fine.
